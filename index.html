Finance analytics

Apple Inc. Financial Performance

Three analysis pages plus an About page on 67 quarters of Apple financials (FY2010 to FY2026 Q3) from SEC filings. Python reshaped 25,135 raw rows into one row per quarter, removed duplicates, derived Q4 and adjusted per-share data for the 2014 and 2020 stock splits.

Question: How did Apple's revenue, margins, costs and cash returns change from FY2010 to FY2025?

Findings: Revenue grew from $65B to $416B, gross margin rose from 39.4% to 46.9%, and cash returned reached 107% of free cash flow in FY2025.
